A consulting practice helping teams apply AI, modern software engineering, and open ways of working - from strategy through hands-on implementation.

What it is

Lontra Labs is an independent consultancy founded and run by Russell Rutledge. The work spans strategy and hands-on building: helping teams decide where AI, better engineering practices, and open collaboration actually pay off, then doing the work to get there.

The aim is simple - better software, and better decisions about how to build it.

How it helps

  • AI strategy and implementation - finding where AI creates real leverage, then building it into how a team works.
  • InnerSource and open source - applying open-source ways of working inside organizations.
  • Engineering leadership - fractional and advisory support for teams and the people who lead them.
  • Developer culture and platform - the practices and tooling that let engineers do their best work.
